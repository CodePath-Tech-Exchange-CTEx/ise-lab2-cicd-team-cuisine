# Usability Test Report

## Overview
This report captures the Friends feature usability results from the April 3 usability session, plus the Music feature usability plan and placeholders for Kameron’s final results.

## Friends Feature Usability Test

### Test purpose
Evaluate whether users can easily find the bet with the most friends, understand bet details, and identify whether they want to place a bet.

### Participants
- Testee #1: JG
- Testee #2: JW

### Test setup
- Introduce self and the team.
- Ask about prior experience with similar betting apps.
- Give the task: "Find the bet with the most friends betting on it and get more info about that bet so you can decide whether to place a bet."
- Ask follow-up questions: "Any improvements you’d like to see? Anything confusing? Anything you wish was there? What was good about the app?"

### Task scenario
1. Open the main bet feed.
2. Find the bet with the highest number of friends.
3. Review the bet details and decide whether you would want to bet on it.
4. Explain what made the bet easy or hard to find.

### JG results
- Prior experience: Uses betting apps like PrizePicks, but has never used anything like Kalshi.
- Expected behavior: Swipe/scroll down, then click the bet.
- Confusion: None.
- Suggestions: None.
- Positive feedback: Liked the clear information presented on the page.

### JW results
- Prior experience: No previous experience with betting apps.
- Expected behavior: Click each bet for more info, review who is betting, and compare bets.
- Confusion: Needed a clearer scroll cue.
- Feedback:
  - Add a scroll indicator or visual hint that more bets are available below the fold.
  - Sort bets by the highest number of friends so the most social bets are easiest to find.
  - Liked that the bet cards show clear information before clicking, reducing the need to open every bet.

### Key feedback and fixes
- Add a scroll indicator or prompt on the bet feed.
- Add sorting by friend count, with the highest number of friends first.
- Keep bet summary cards rich and easy to scan.

### Prototype changes
- Before: The bet feed lacked a clear scroll cue and had no friend-first ordering.
- After: Updated paper prototypes should show a scroll hint and a "Most friends" sort option, plus visible friend counts on each bet card.

## Music Feature Usability Test

### Plan status
Kameron is conducting the Music feature usability test for the team. The final results will be updated here when available.

### Test purpose
Evaluate whether users can discover music content, understand the Music feature layout, and use music details and playback controls.

### Test setup
- Introduce self and the team.
- Ask about prior experience with music discovery apps.
- Give the task: "Use the Music feature to find a song or playlist that fits your mood, and open the details so you can decide whether to listen to it."

### Task scenario
1. Open the Music feature landing screen.
2. Browse by genre, mood, or trending recommendations.
3. Select a music item to view details.
4. Evaluate the clarity of the controls and the usefulness of the information.

### Notes to capture
- How easy is it to find a music recommendation?
- Does the interface clearly show what to tap next?
- Do users understand the playback controls and preview options?
- Is the music detail view easy to read and compare?
- Are there any missing features users expect (e.g. playlists, favorites, preview play)?

### Expected results
- Users should be able to discover recommended music quickly.
- The Music feature should make discovery feel natural and easy to scan.
- The details page should explain why the item is recommended and how to play it.
- Any confusion or missing interaction should be documented with direct quotes.
